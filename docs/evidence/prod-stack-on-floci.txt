Notes:
1. aws_opensearch_domain: Floci runs a real OpenSearch container per domain and needs the Docker socket.
   Our Floci container does not mount /var/run/docker.sock (security choice), so the domain never left 'processing'.
   The apply was stopped after ~4 minutes of waiting.
2. aws_s3_bucket tags: the DenyInsecureTransport bucket policy is enforced by Floci and the endpoint is plain HTTP,
   so the provider's tag read was denied. On AWS the provider uses HTTPS and the policy allows it.
   This is why envs/floci-local sets enforce_tls_bucket_policy = false.
